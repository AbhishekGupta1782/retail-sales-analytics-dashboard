# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing retail sales performance,
profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional
trends.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an Excel-based data
analytics project designed to transform raw retail data into meaningful
business insights.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance
using interactive charts, KPIs, and slicers. It helps identify business
trends, profitable products, and regional and customer performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

-   Analyze overall sales and profitability.
-   Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
-   Identify top-performing products and sub-categories.
-   Analyze sales trends over different years.
-   Compare regional and state-wise sales performance.
-   Understand customer segment contributions.
-   Evaluate shipping methods and product categories.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

  Visualization           Description
  ----------------------- ---------------------------------------------
  Sales Distribution      Sales distribution across product sizes
  Sales by Segment        Consumer and Corporate performance
  Sales Trend Over Time   Year-wise sales and profit analysis
  Sales by Region         Comparison of regional sales
  Sales by City Type      Sales across Tier 1, Tier 2 and Village
  Top 10 Sub-Categories   Highest-performing sub-categories by profit
  Top 10 Products         Top products based on sales
  Order by Ship Mode      Analysis of different shipping methods
  Sales by Category       Category-wise sales performance
  Revenue by State        State-wise revenue comparison

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive slicers to explore business
performance:

-   **Year:** Filter sales by year.
-   **Region:** Analyze regional performance.
-   **Segment:** Compare customer segments.
-   **Category:** Explore product category performance.
